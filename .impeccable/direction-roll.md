## The rut, named and excluded

The page this category always ships: a dark hero with a stock photograph of a
smiling man in hi-vis beside an open bonnet, a red CALL NOW band, three icon
cards and a "why choose us" tick list. Its predictable opposite: a white Swiss
grid page with a huge phone number and a great deal of air. Neither entered the
seven.

Also excluded, as worlds already spent on this client's sibling sites: club
motorsport livery, car care tin packaging, UK vehicle paperwork, car show
judging ephemera, the motor factor trade counter, the Potteries in Spode cobalt,
mid century AA and RAC roadside patrol, the diagnostic instrument panel, the
Raleigh head badge, and the CB radio night dispatch log.

## The seven, derived from Derby's own world, ordered by resonance

1. Derwent Valley Mills on the night shift. The first place in the world to run
   three shifts through the night.
2. The Rolls-Royce aero engine test house data card. Derby is where the Trent
   is built.
3. The Toyota Burnaston andon board. A line stops, a light calls for help.
4. **Midland Railway signalling.** Derby is the Midland's own works town, and
   signalling is the one trade whose entire point is that somebody is awake at
   three in the morning. **Assigned by the roll.**
5. Robert Bakewell's wrought ironwork in Derby Cathedral.
6. The Royal Crown Derby backstamp and its Imari palette.
7. The Derby County ram.

Families spanned: industrial architecture, engineering documentation, factory
process notation, transport signage, forged metal, ceramic decoration, sport
identity.

## The hand

Three challengers were dealt and fused before judging:

- **Bedside 3AM** (seven segment display family). Fused hard: the visitor is
  looking at red digits at 3am when the car dies. Lost on breadth, because
  nothing longer than a number can be set in the face, and red glow on near
  black is the most crowded look in the category.
- **Night Drive Grid** (receding wireframe grid and banded sun). Fused halfway:
  night driving is the visitor's real scene, but the world promises a music
  video.
- **Scroller Queue** (cracktro title screen). Fused poorly: the demoscene is not
  this audience's world, and enclosure free bitmap text fights the one thing the
  page must do.

Left in the re-roll pool: the Dong Ho shell ground woodblock print, the black
hole accretion disk, and the curved crease paper shell.

## The verdict

Weighed on audience identification and product clarity, Signal Box held. It wins
identification on locality and ties on clarity, because a signal that comes off
danger is exactly what the visitor wants to happen.

## What the user chose, and then unchose

The decision page offered the assigned direction, the three fused challengers,
and the standing canon exit (the dark professional trade page the ten sibling
sites run). The user took **the roll**, not canon, which was the first time in
eleven builds in this series that canon had not been chosen.

Signal Box was then built in full, taken through a finish review and two
verdict rounds, and shipped. Seeing it live, the user asked for **something
more professional**, which is the standing exit taken in plain words. The world
was replaced wholesale rather than polished: convention became the commitment,
the craft bar is the client's own showroomdetailing and CJValeting builds, and
the accent, copper, was the client's choice.

The lesson for the next one in this series: the roll can win the decision page
and still lose the client. Offering it stays right, and the record of the
choice matters, but for these trade clients the built artifact is the only
review that counts. Canon is now recorded as a brand commitment in PRODUCT.md.
